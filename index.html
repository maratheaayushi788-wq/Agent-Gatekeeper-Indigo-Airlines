IndiGo Gatekeeper Governance Policy Matrix

MBA Architectural Framework: Autonomous AI Risk Mitigation

RULE 1 ALLOW
Registered active agents requesting read-only fare viewing are ALLOWED.
Risk Level: Zero. Read-only operations do not modify booking state, alter inventory, or charge credit accounts. Safe for autonomous AI handling.
RULE 2 BLOCK
Unknown or unregistered agents are BLOCKED.
Risk Level: Critical. Unverified bots attempting to interface with airline APIs are rejected immediately to prevent scraping, credential stuffing, and bot attacks.
RULE 3 ASK CUSTOMER
Flight booking requests require customer confirmation and return ASK CUSTOMER.
Risk Level: High Financial. Booking binds seat inventory, initiates payment charges, and issues legal passenger contracts. Human consent is legally and commercially mandatory.
RULE 4 ASK CUSTOMER
Flight change requests require customer confirmation and return ASK CUSTOMER.
Risk Level: High Itinerary. Re-routing or modifying dates affects flight schedules, baggage transfers, and modification penalties. AI cannot unilaterally alter customer journeys.
RULE 5 ALLOW
Refunds of ₹5,000 or below from registered active agents are ALLOWED.
Risk Level: Low Financial (Micro-refund). Cancellation of minor ancillary fees or budget sectors within ₹5,000 is auto-approved to streamline airline operations and customer NPS.
RULE 6 ASK CUSTOMER
Refunds above ₹5,000 require customer confirmation and return ASK CUSTOMER.
Risk Level: Medium-High Financial. Substantial refunds above ₹5,000 trigger a human validation gate to eliminate unauthorized cancellations or rogue AI actions.
RULE 7 BLOCK
Revoked agents are always BLOCKED, regardless of requested action.
Risk Level: Severe (Compromised Credential). Once a customer or security officer revokes an agent, access is irreversibly disabled with audit timestamping.
RULE 8 BLOCK
Any unauthorized or unsupported action is BLOCKED.
Risk Level: High (Security Perimeter). Principle of Least Privilege: Any API verb or payload outside defined airline services is denied by default.
